# Faction Tactics — roadmap (locked 2026-09-18)

| Version | Scope | Notes |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **v1.0** | Enemy behavior only | Doctrine packs, squad FSM, Siege Assault, optional ambience (fog/message). No territory. No friendly NPCs. Behavior-only encounter rate. |
| **v2.0** | Territory claiming | Enclose-to-claim (walls + dual-use vanilla wards), clans, map borders, FT safe-zone / Assault frontier hooks. See [TERRITORY-ONEPAGER.md](TERRITORY-ONEPAGER.md). |
| **v3.0** | Living settlements | Villages / cities, peasants/villagers, guards, recruit & command — huge NPC overhaul on top of claimed fiefs. |
| **v4.0** (notional) | Faction standings | Ally/enemy reputation with world factions; impacts trade access, villager production, and who assaults your walls. |

Do not start v2 until v1 AI playtest signs off. Do not start v3 until v2 claim loop feels good. v4 stays notional until v3 settlements exist.

Workshop notes: [V3-SETTLEMENTS-WORKSHOP.md](V3-SETTLEMENTS-WORKSHOP.md)
